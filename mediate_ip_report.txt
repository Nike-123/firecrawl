3. WHY IT IS 43 AND NOT 76 (honest limitation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The search page renders only ~25 "Featured" profiles per non-JavaScript request
and puts the remaining results behind a JavaScript "Load More" button. The order
is RANDOMIZED on every request, so normal pagination does not enumerate the set.

Tested and rejected as non-working: paged, page, pg, offset, start, per_page,
posts_per_page, limit, count, number, display, results, num, show, show_all,
all, load_more, plus the lname filter (which dropped the IP filter entirely and
returned 364 unrelated mediators). None changed the count or produced a stable
second page. Repeated loads yielded ~32 unique featured profiles.

11 more IP profiles were recovered from search-engine indexes of mediate.com;
each was fetched and its "Intellectual Property" practice area confirmed before
inclusion. ~33 of the 76 could not be enumerated without executing the site's
JavaScript, which the available fetch tool does not do. This is reported, not
hidden: the sheet does NOT claim all 76 were collected.
